01 — The Gap

Industry runs on hardware.
Classrooms teach theory.

Business and industry are changing rapidly thanks to AI and robotics. The demand for graduates with genuine hardware skills has also risen sharply but in higher education, those skills aren't easy to develop.

Classroom learning is mostly theoretical and its not often that students get the opportunity to work on real high end hardware used in industry. The equipment required to close that gap is simply too expensive.

Without massive investments in this kind of infrastructure, UFV graduates face a widening gap in competancy the moment they enter enterprise technical environments.

US$200,000

The cost of a single eight-GPU server built on NVIDIA H200 NVL cards, which run over US$25,000 each and draw 5-7 kW under load.[6]

Few universities can afford equipment at that price point. ARIC is designed so that UFV can.

02 — The Proposal

One facility, three commitments

A multidisciplinary computing hub, responding to the rise of AI, high-performance computing and physical robotics.

01

Physical infrastructure

A new multi-story building built on a separate 151.31 acre site, approximately 15 minutes from the main campus. The facility will feature specialized high-density server rooms, AI software labs, and interactive robotics assembly and testing arenas, all of which will be LEED Gold certified.

02

Enterprise Hardware Infrastructure

Advanced GPU clusters, local high throughput storage networks, and industrial and educational robotics hardware, including automated logistics hardware, AMO robotics, and service robots for enterprise applications.

03

Applied Curriculum Integration

Co-development of a specialization track in undergraduate education and applied certification courses related to the deployment of AI systems, hardware configuration, and the integration of humans and robots in business environments.

03 — The Return

What $100M buys beyond a building

Industry Applied Research Revenue

Identify strategic investment opportunities and research collaborations with industrial, agricultural, logistics, and other industry partners throughout the Fraser Valley that are interested in developing custom solutions through applied research in automation and related technologies.

Student Recruitment & Enrollment Growth

Expand domestic and international enrollment across multiple disciplines and departments, including CIS and STEM programs, by providing students with access to world-class laboratory and research equipment.

Graduate Market Value

Position UFV as a pipeline for technology companies and other organizations looking for workforce-ready systems administrators, AI specialists, computing professionals, and robotics integration engineers.

04 — The Site

151.31 acres on Huntingdon Road

A parcel of land 28310 Huntingdon Road, Abbotsford which represents more than double the footprint of the current UFV Abbotsford campus. A roughly a fifteen-minute drive from it.

This land allows for High-density server halls, robotics testing arenas and the power and cooling plant they depend on which would be difficult to add to an existing campus. A dedicatd site allows the facility to be build correctly from the ground up with room for future expansion.

Acquisition cost: $16.85M of the $100M budget.

05 — The Timeline

Four years, with responsible kill points built in

Four kill points stand between approval and the September 2030 deadline. If the project cannot clear one, it is redesigned, delayed or stopped.

  1. Kill Point 1

    Feasibility

    Can UFV realistically build and operate the centre within the time and the $100M available? If not, redesign or terminate.

  2. Kill Point 2

    Public consultation

    Surveys, public meetings and stakeholder sessions test community support before the design is finalised. Unresolvable opposition stops the project.

  3. Kill Point 3

    Design & budget approval

    Building layout, laboratory requirements, HPC infrastructure and updated cost estimates reviewed against the budget.

  4. Kill Point 4

    Technology installation

    Networking, robotics and laboratory systems verified against technical requirements before full commissioning.
